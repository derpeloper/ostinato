# privacy policy

this privacy policy explains how i handle, use, and protect your information when you use the ostinato bot.

## data collection and use

ostinato processes all voice synthesis requests on my host machine. any data you provide to the bot — including text prompts, generated audio files, and server configurations — is processed solely on my hosting device.  

i do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of this data with any third party or external server. text prompts are processed to generate audio in real-time and are not stored permanently. server-specific preferences (such as voice, speed, and language settings) are stored locally on my machine using a file-based sqlite database to keep your configuration persistent across sessions.

## data security

your server configuration data is managed within a local sqlite database on my host machine. as the operator of the official instance, i take appropriate measures to secure my hosting environment. since ostinato operates locally on my end without any cloud-based database services or outbound telemetry, your sensitive text prompts never leave my server and are kept secure from external exposure.

## policy updates

i may update this privacy policy from time to time. i will notify you of any changes by updating this document or posting a notice in my official repository.
